# Connected App — Concept, Card Vault & Curated Packs

## Core Interaction Architecture: "Create First, Then Slide & Swipe Together"

Instead of an app that constantly interrupts conversation with typing and reading, the experience separates **Creation** from **Connection**:

1. **Pick One of the 5 Shared Experiences:**
   - One person picks the pack for the table (1 card, 3 cards, 4 cards, 5 cards, or a locked "Pick Your Round" pack) so **both people always go through the exact same shared sequence**.
2. **Phase 1 — The Creation Sprint (1–4 minutes):**
   - Each person holds their own phone and rapidly builds their cards under playful time limits (60s finger sketch, 45s photo scavenger, 30s emoji pitch, etc.).
   - The time pressure keeps everything spontaneous, unpolished, and funny—no overthinking or curating allowed.
3. **Phase 2 — The Slide & Swipe (The Real Conversation):**
   - Once both decks are ready, **you physically slide your phone across the table** to the other person (or sync both phones into a single shared deck).
   - You go through the cards **one by one together**, swiping to advance at your own pace, telling the stories behind each card, and laughing together.
4. **Phase 3 — The Keepsake Capsule:**
   - Attach quick voice notes or one-line captions to the cards and save the night's deck as a shared digital keepsake.

## The 5 Experience Packs (How Cards Are Packaged)

To protect the **shared experience** and prevent "menu fatigue" at the table, both people always play the exact same sequence of cards.

### Option 1: "The Cocktail Napkin" (1 Card)
- **Creation Time:** 60 seconds | **Conversation Time:** 5–10 mins
- **Best For:** Right as you sit down, waiting for drinks/coffee, or testing the waters with zero commitment.
- **The Lineup:**
  1. **The 60-Second Napkin Sketch** *(Draw something you want to show the other person)*

---

### Option 2: "The Quick Spark" (3 Cards)
- **Creation Time:** ~2 minutes | **Conversation Time:** 15–25 mins
- **Best For:** Coffee dates, casual first meetups, or a fast, high-energy warm-up.
- **Why This Arc Works:** Moves from **Show $\rightarrow$ Guess $\rightarrow$ Reflect** using three totally different formats (drawing, emojis, and a personal prompt).
- **The Lineup:**
  1. **The 60-Second Napkin Sketch** *(Disarming visual opener — melts the ego immediately)*
  2. **The 3-Emoji Story Pitch** *(Playful guessing game that unlocks a funny/wild true story)*
  3. **Dealer's Choice** *(Pick 1 Joe Hudson-spun icebreaker to land on a warm, genuine note)*

---

### Option 3: "The First Date Classic" (4 Cards)
- **Creation Time:** ~3 minutes | **Conversation Time:** 30–45 mins
- **Best For:** First dates over drinks or dinner where you want laughter, a sense of who they are in the world, and playful banter.
- **Why This Arc Works:** Adds **place** and **playful friction** before ending with a side-by-side comparison of how your two brains work.
- **The Lineup:**
  1. **The 60-Second Napkin Sketch** *(Draw something you want to show me)*
  2. **Drop a Pin ("Take Me Somewhere")** *(Spin the globe & walk me around one spot on Earth that matters to you)*
  3. **The Spicy Hill Slider** *(Harmless unpopular opinion — Agree or Fight Me)*
  4. **Same Prompt, Two Brains** *(Both react to the exact same visual prompt, revealed side-by-side at the end)*

---

### Option 4: "The Full Table / Deep Dive" (5 Cards)
- **Creation Time:** ~4 minutes | **Conversation Time:** 45–90 mins (carries a whole evening)
- **Best For:** When the vibe is already good and you want to go from laughing hard to really knowing what makes each other tick.
- **Why This Arc Works:** Covers present life, real photos, past memories, future dreams, and playful intuition about each other.
- **The Lineup:**
  1. **The 60-Second Napkin Sketch** *(The signature opener)*
  2. **Show, Don't Tell (Camera Roll Scavenger)** *(A real photo from your life that needs a 30-second backstory)*
  3. **The Pie Chart of My Brain** *(What has actually been occupying your head this week)*
  4. **Two Memories & A Daydream** *(Two real past stories + one secret future wish to guess)*
  5. **The Playful Gut-Read** *(Lighthearted first-impression guesses about each other)*

---

### Option 5: "Table Mix" (Controlled Custom / Shuffle Mode)
- **How It Protects the Shared Experience:**
  - If you let each person pick different cards independently, you lose the magic of comparing answers. And if you show 20 toggles, people spend 5 minutes configuring instead of connecting.
  - Instead, **one person sets the Table Mix in 10 seconds** (or taps a **"Shuffle 3"** dice button), and **both phones lock into that exact same card order**:
    - **Slot 1 (Locked Anchor):** Always starts with **The 60-Second Napkin Sketch**.
    - **Slots 2–4 (Tap up to 3 from the Vault, or tap "Surprise Us"):** Pick any 2 or 3 extras from the 11-card vault (e.g., *The 30-Second Soundtrack*, *Pie Chart*, *Dealer's Choice*, *Drop a Pin*) so you can replay the app on a second or third hangout with fresh cards.

## The Complete 11-Card Vault Reference

1. **The 60-Second Napkin Sketch:** 60s finger-drawing of something you want to show the other person + story.
2. **Show, Don't Tell (Camera Roll Scavenger):** 45s to pick a photo from your camera roll that needs a 30-second backstory.
3. **Drop a Pin ("Take Me Somewhere"):** 30s to drop a pin anywhere on Earth and give a Street View / satellite tour.
4. **Two Memories & A Daydream:** Three quick headlines (two real past stories, one wish/dream for the other person to guess).
5. **The Playful Gut-Read:** Harmless first-impression guesses about the other person (*"As a 10-year-old, I bet you were..."*).
6. **The 30-Second Soundtrack:** Queue a song that matches your week or a great memory; plays a 25s chorus snippet.
7. **The 3-Emoji Story Pitch:** Summarize a wild story or your ideal weekend in 3 emojis; they guess before you tell it.
8. **The Pie Chart of My Brain:** Slice a circle with your finger into 3–4 wedges showing what's actually in your head lately.
9. **The Spicy Hill Slider:** A harmless unpopular opinion + heat meter; they tap *Agree* or *Fight Me*.
10. **Same Prompt, Two Brains:** Both people get the exact same playful prompt and react in their own way; revealed side-by-side.
11. **Dealer's Choice (Joe Hudson Spin):** Pick 1 prompt from a grid of 8 classic icebreakers spun to spark real stories instead of rehearsed answers:
    - *"The best advice I ever got—or the 'good advice' I'm happiest I ignored."*
    - *"One life cheat-code I had to learn the hard way."*
    - *"Something I used to judge or dislike that I now genuinely love."*
    - *"A 'failure' or detour that turned out to be one of the best things that happened to me."*
    - *"Something that makes me completely lose track of time."*
    - *"The fastest way to make me feel completely at home (or how to tell when I'm genuinely excited)."*
    - *"Something true about me that almost nobody guesses on a first meeting."*
    - *"A free or $5 pleasure in life that I take way too seriously."*
